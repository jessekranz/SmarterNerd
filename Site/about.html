Jesse Kranz

From military communications to managing $32M in technology infrastructure. Jesse spent 12 years as a Chief Information Officer, leading technology strategy for major organizations. In that role, he designed, deployed, and managed systems that ran critical operations — with the same precision discipline that kept him and his team alive in combat.

When he transitioned to the private sector, Jesse realized something: most small businesses can't afford a CIO. They're stuck between freelancers who treat each project like a one-off, and agencies that pass them to junior staff. So he started SmarterNerd — to bring CIO-grade discipline, strategy, and execution to South Florida businesses.

Today, Jesse doesn't just oversee projects. He builds them. He codes. He audits. He's the first person on the call with new clients, and he stays involved through delivery. That's founder-led. That's senior-only. No hand-offs.
