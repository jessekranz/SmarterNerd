Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Do you offer payment plans? +

Yes. Projects can be split 50/50 (half upfront, half on delivery). Recurring services require month-to-month or annual commitment, but you can cancel anytime. For large projects, we can discuss milestone-based payments.

What if I cancel mid-month? +

We'll prorate the refund. If you cancel with 15 days left in the month, we refund the prorated amount. No penalties. We'd rather keep you happy than lock you into something you don't need.

Do hosting/domain costs scale? +

Domain & hosting are minimal and fixed: ~$15-30/month total. We include 3 months of hosting with website builds, then you pay for your own. We recommend reliable providers and help with setup.

What if AI service doesn't deliver results? +

30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not seeing value in the first month, full refund. No questions asked. We're confident our AI chatbots and automation work, but we want you risk-free.

Can you work within my budget? +

Absolutely. Let's talk. We have entry points at every budget level. If you need something custom, we can scope it and quote. Call or email Jesse — he'll find a way forward if there's a mutual fit.

Do you have discounts for long-term contracts? +

Yes. Annual prepay saves 15% on recurring services. Multi-service bundles also get discounts. For example, web design + SEO + AI together can save you 10-20%. Ask during your consultation.

What's included in "priority support"? +

Priority support (+$200/mo): 24-hour response guarantee, dedicated Slack channel, monthly strategy calls, and direct line to Jesse. Recommended for time-sensitive campaigns.

Can I scale up or down services? +

Yes. Move from Starter to Professional AI? No problem, we prorate. Need to pause the chatbot for a month? Cancel and restart anytime. We scale with your business, not the other way around.
