Contact & Consultation FAQ

How long until I hear back? +

Within 24 hours, usually faster. Jesse personally reviews every inquiry. If you need a quicker response, send an email or schedule a call directly via Calendly.

Do you work with my industry? +

We work across legal, healthcare, e-commerce, retail, services, and more. If you're a South Florida business that needs web, SEO, or AI — we can help. Let's talk about your specific situation.

Can you fix my current website? +

Yes. We do redesigns, performance audits, SEO overhauls, and migrations. Send us details about what you need, and we'll review and quote. Some work is quick, some requires deeper rebuilds.

What's the first step? +

1) Fill out the form or email Jesse directly. 2) He reviews your situation and reaches out with initial thoughts. 3) Schedule a call to discuss strategy and timeline. 4) Get a custom proposal.

Do you offer retainers? +

Yes. We offer monthly retainers for AI services, SEO, and ongoing support. Most retainers start at $400+/month depending on scope. You can add or remove services, and cancel anytime.

What if I just want a consultation? +

That's free. Book a 15-minute call. Jesse will ask about your goals, challenges, and opportunities. You'll get honest feedback on what's possible and what it might cost. No pressure, no sales pitch.

Do you do custom projects? +

Absolutely. If you need something outside our standard offerings, we scope it and quote. Send details and we'll discuss feasibility, timeline, and cost.

How does the free consultation work? +

Book a 15-30 minute call directly via Calendly (link in the contact box). Or email Jesse to set up a time. He'll ask questions, listen to your situation, and share preliminary thoughts on strategy and options. Zero obligations.
