Common Questions

Will QR codes work on my print materials?

+

Yes. QR codes work on any printed material: business cards, brochures, billboards, packaging, direct mail. Smartphones can scan them natively.

Can I track who scans the code?

+

Yes. We track scan volume, location (city-level), device type, and timestamp. You will not know individual identities, but you will know campaign performance.

Can I change where the code points?

+

Yes. You can update the destination URL anytime without reprinting. Perfect for seasonal campaigns or A/B testing.

Will they work on old smartphones?

+

Most smartphones built in the last 10 years can scan QR codes natively. Nearly 100% of iPhone and Android phones from the last 5 years support them.

Can the code be customized with my brand?

+

Yes. We design codes that maintain your brand colors and style while staying fully functional and scannable.

What can the code link to?

+

Any URL. Your website, a landing page, a form, a calendar booking page, a video, a phone number, an email, a text message, or a PDF.
